About Venus Medical
& Dental Centre

From humble beginnings as a small medical and dental surgery in Avondale suburb in Harare, Venus Medical and Dental Centre has blossomed into a beacon of comprehensive healthcare. Founded by Dr Tsungu in 2014, our clinic has grown significantly, evolving from a modest practice into a multifaceted facility offering a wide range of services.

This growth has been fueled not only by our commitment to excellence but also by the unwavering support and trust of our clients. Our patients have been integral to our journey, and we are honoured to have been part of their families' growth and well-being.

As part of the Venus Healthcare Services Private Ltd, we have continually expanded and specialised our medical and dental services. Today, our team of specialists in various fields ensures that every patient receives the highest quality of care. At Venus Medical and Dental Centre, we are committed to maintaining our legacy of excellence and innovation in healthcare.
