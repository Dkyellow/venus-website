About Us

Dr Tsungu, the director is a general dental practitioner with over 10 years experience. He has a special interest in Orthodontics & Implantology. He holds a Bachelor of Dental Surgery from the University of Zimbabwe & has a certificate in orthodontics with McGann Postgraduate School of Dentistry in the United States of America. Dr Tsungu also acquired a certificate in Dental Implantology at University of Pretoria.

Dr Tsungu
