"In a caring manner, with compassion"

At Venus Medical & Dental Centre, we offer a range of multidisciplinary healthcare services across medical, dental, mental health and specialist care. By bringing these services together under one roof, we aim to make it easier for patients and families to access the healthcare support they need in one place. We believe that healthcare is not always limited to one area of concern. Our team works across different disciplines to support the varied and interconnected health needs of our patients, while maintaining a focus on individual care and professional practice.

Frequently Asked
Questions

Trusted medical, dental and mental healthcare for families in Harare.

Do I need to make an appointment, or I can walk in?

Yes. Appointments are required to ensure that a dedicated time slot is reserved for you.

To book, click here or contact us.

How do I schedule my appointment?

You can:

Book online - click here.

Call or WhatsApp. For medical bookings contact: +263 77 489 0158 | medical@venushealthcare.co.zw

Dental bookings: +263 77 728 9797 | dental@venushealthcare.co.zw

Visit us - Our team can assist you with your booking in the clinic.

Do you accept medical aid?

Yes, we accept a range of medical aids. Terms and conditions apply depending on the service you require and your medical aid plan. Contact us to find out more details.

What are your office hours?

We open 8am – 5pm on weekdays Monday to Friday.
8am to 1pm on Saturdays.
Sundays and Public Holidays we are closed.

What time does the GP start?

The GP starts at 10am – 4:30pm on weekdays and on Saturday he starts at 9am – 12:30pm.
